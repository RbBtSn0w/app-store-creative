# PRD-001 pilot protocol

Status: prepared; no participants recruited or results collected.

## Required inputs

The product owner supplies authorized English/Chinese iPhone and Mac captures for six meaningful scenes, identifies the existing production workflow, and recruits ten consenting participants representative of the intended independent developer audience. Use participant codes; do not record account credentials or unnecessary personal data.

## Study procedure

1. Review real fixtures for correct product state, language, readable copy and permission to use them. Preserve original source bytes.
2. Have each participant complete the same six-card, two-language, two-target task with their current workflow and Studio. Counterbalance workflow order to reduce learning effects. Record tool versions and assistance.
3. Measure elapsed task time from the start of ready-environment setup through finding and reviewing all 24 valid outputs, including setup, repairs and rendering. Record active editing and setup time separately as diagnostic measures; they do not replace the PRD elapsed-time metric. Record abandoned and failed sessions with elapsed time and reason. Unassisted completion requires correct captures, readable copy, complete matrix and fresh local evidence.
4. Include one requested copy repair and save/reopen task. Observe errors, recovery, unexpected inheritance and confusion about local verification versus upload. Stop if the participant wishes to withdraw.
5. Ask whether the participant would use the result for a real release, what blocked trust, and their satisfaction using a fixed five-point scale. Retain specific comments with consent.
6. Follow up after the next real release opportunity. Record whether Studio was used and why. Do not equate intent with retention.

Compare paired elapsed task times and completion; report every failure and denominator, including abandoned sessions. Report the completer median alongside completion rate and failed-session times. At the 30-day follow-up, record invitations, returns, attempts, completions and abandonment separately; the repeat-task completion denominator includes all participants who start the follow-up. Treat PRD targets (8/10 completion, at most 15 minutes, at least 30% faster) as hypotheses until measured. A small pilot supports workflow decisions, not statistically proven conversion uplift. Prioritize observed blocking issues before accepting the product release gates.
